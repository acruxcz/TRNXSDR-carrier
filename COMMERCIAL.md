# Commercial Licensing

This hardware design is dual-licensed. While the open source version is
available under [CERN-OHL-S v2](https://ohwr.org/cern_ohl_s_v2.txt),
a commercial license is available for integrators who need to incorporate
this design into proprietary products.

---

## Who needs a commercial license?

You need a commercial license if you:

- Integrate this design (or derivatives) into a product whose hardware
  design files you do not intend to make publicly available
- Build a product based on this design for sale without releasing your
  modifications under CERN-OHL-S
- Require contractual warranties, indemnification, or SLA beyond what
  the open source license provides

You **do not** need a commercial license if you:

- Use this design for personal, research, or educational purposes
- Build products and release all hardware design files (schematics,
  layout, BOM, production files) under CERN-OHL-S v2
- Evaluate the design before deciding on licensing

---

## What the commercial license includes

- Right to incorporate this design into proprietary hardware products
  without triggering CERN-OHL-S reciprocity requirements
- Right to modify the design without obligation to publish modifications
- Access to design support and engineering consultation (scope per agreement)
- Warranty and liability terms beyond the open source disclaimer
- Optionally: priority access to new hardware revisions and roadmap input

## Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

All contributors to this project must sign a Contributor License Agreement
before their contributions can be accepted. The CLA grants the project
maintainer the right to sublicense contributions under commercial terms,
while contributors retain their copyright.

CLA available at: www.tronex.cz/cla

This is a prerequisite for the dual licensing model to remain legally sound.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I evaluate before deciding on a license?**  
Yes. Evaluation and prototyping under CERN-OHL-S v2 is always free.
A commercial license is only required before moving to production with
a closed design.

**What if I release my product as open hardware?**  
If you publish all hardware design files under CERN-OHL-S v2, no
commercial license is needed. The open source path is always available.

**What happens to contributions I make under CLA?**  
You retain copyright of your contributions. The CLA grants the maintainer
the right to include your contributions in commercially licensed versions.
Your contributions remain available to the community under CERN-OHL-S v2.

**I'm a small company / startup — is there a reduced rate?**  
Yes. Contact us to discuss terms appropriate for your situation.
